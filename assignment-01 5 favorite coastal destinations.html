My Top 5 Favorite Coastal Destinations

  1. 1. Mendocino, California

    Famous for beautiful drive there as drive through the Califronia Redwoods.The town is charming and famous for being the opening seen to the show Murder She Wrote. It is also known as the Cape Cod of the West Coast.

  2. 2.Maui, Hawaii

    Famous for its dramatic volcanic coastline, golden sand beaches, and scenic ocean drives along the coast

  3. 3. Orange Beach, Alabama

    Renowned for clear emerald green waters, sugar sand beaches, southern hospitality and laid back coastal living.

  4. 4. Deerfield Beach, Florida

    Most like Maui weather and ocean temperature on the Atlantic coast, dramatic ocean views and great boardwalk, and they have the BEST 4th of JULY celebration and fireworks.

  5. 5. Monterey Bay, California

    Offers sweeping Pacific ocean views, frolicing sea otters, seals and sea lions & known vibrant marine sanctuaries, and beautiful coastal walking trails.
